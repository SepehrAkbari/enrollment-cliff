(TCC Owasso Campus Facebook) OWASSO, Okla. — Tulsa Community College (TCC) announced they will be no longer offering classes at its Owasso Community Campus following the end of the spring 2024 semester. TCC said the decision was made due to several factors including enrollment patterns and course offerings. TCC leased the space at the Owasso Campus from Tulsa Tech. Tulsa Tech said its Owasso Campus and Conference Center will remain open and continue to provide Tulsa Tech classes. The full statement from TCC can be seen below: “After careful deliberation, Tulsa Community College has made the challenging decision to discontinue course offerings on the Owasso Community Campus beginning with the Summer 2024 semester. This decision is based on an evaluation of several factors, including enrollment patterns, operational effectiveness, course offerings, and alignment with our long-term strategic goals. We believe that reorganizing our resources will enable us to better serve our students and the community.
